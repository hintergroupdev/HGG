Design & Implementation Approach
Please use your professional design judgment for layout, spacing, responsive behavior, typography scale, image placement, transitions, cards, mobile adaptation, and other normal design decisions.
The Home Page should establish the principal visual system for the website before that system is rolled out across the remaining pages.
The overall visual direction should remain premium, modern, minimalist, sophisticated, internationally oriented, Ghanaian-rooted, and understated.
Please do not materially rewrite approved HGG messaging, leadership information, service names, project disclosures, or legal text without HGG approval.
If any section appears too long for an effective layout, please recommend a presentation solution or proposed condensed wording before materially changing the approved copy.
Legal Pages
The Privacy Policy and Terms of Service are currently in a separate HGG Legal Review Package. They should not be treated as final production legal copy until HGG Legal Counsel completes review and HGG provides the approved versions.
You may prepare the Privacy Policy and Terms page layouts using the established website design system, but final live wording must be the counsel-approved text supplied by HGG.
Projects, Partners & Leadership
Do not publish specific project names, partner names/logos, statistics, financial figures, or government/regulatory references unless HGG expressly approves them for public disclosure.
Do not invent executive titles, credentials, biographies, departments, or qualifications.
Use only HGG-approved leadership portraits and final verified public information.
Ownership & Handover
HGG must retain appropriate ownership or administrator-level control of the domain, hosting, DNS, CMS, source-code repository, analytics, Search Console, backups, and critical third-party services. The final website should be transferable to another qualified developer if necessary and should not depend on undocumented vendor-controlled systems.
Please review the attached working documents and identify any missing information, unclear content, technical concerns, design questions, scope questions, or recurring-cost recommendations before making material assumptions.
Thank you again for your professionalism and for working with us to build a website that reflects the direction, credibility, and long-term ambitions of HGG.
