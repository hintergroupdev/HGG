1. PURPOSE OF THIS GUIDE
This guide is the developer-facing implementation document for THE HINTER GROUP GHANA LTD (HGG) corporate website. It consolidates the Quick-Start Developer Brief, approved imagery and visual direction, core technical requirements, selected publication controls, and final handover requirements into one practical working reference.
It is intentionally narrower than HGG's full internal master document. The full Quality-Control record, executive approval sheets, legal-review correspondence, and other internal governance material remain with HGG.
How to Use This Guide
Use the separate HGG Public Website Copy document as the source for visitor-facing copy.
Use this Developer Implementation Guide as the source for design, build, CMS, SEO, accessibility, security, staging, ownership, and handover requirements.
Use only final HGG-approved project, partner, leadership, and legal information.
Where a material change is recommended, present the recommendation to HGG before implementation.

Content Rewriting
Do not materially rewrite approved HGG copy without approval. If content appears too long for a proposed layout:
Identify the presentation issue.
Recommend a condensed treatment or interface pattern.
Provide the proposed revised wording if a rewrite is necessary.
Obtain HGG approval.
Implement the approved treatment.
Appropriate presentation tools may include accordions, tabs, cards, anchor navigation, progressive disclosure, summaries with 'learn more' treatment, and reusable CMS detail templates.

11. PAGE-SPECIFIC VISUAL DIRECTION
Home
Establish the principal visual system for the website.
Hero should communicate premium corporate positioning, international perspective, confidence, and professionalism.
Use strong typography, generous spacing, restrained motion, and clear calls to action.
A subtle Ghana-to-international connectivity concept may be explored if executed with restraint.
About Us
Communicate trust, stability, professionalism, and long-term vision.
Mission, Vision, and Values should be structured clearly and remain easy to scan.
The six-stage Strategic Approach — IDENTIFY → EVALUATE → CONNECT → COORDINATE → ADVANCE → CREATE VALUE — should be treated as a signature visual element.
Leadership
Authoritative, elegant, highly professional presentation.
Use natural executive portraits and generous spacing.
The Chairman & Founder profile should be the principal executive profile.
Avoid visual effects that turn leadership presentation into personal promotion.
Expertise / Our Services
Use a clear modular structure such as refined service cards, anchors, or expandable sections.
Keep service names and descriptions consistent with approved HGG terminology.
Do not create regulated financial, legal, engineering, architectural, or other professional-service implications that are not approved.
Projects & Strategic Partnerships
Emphasize collaboration, project-development process, and partnership architecture.
Do not fabricate sample projects.
If no specific project is approved for launch, the page should still work strongly as an HGG project and partnership framework.
Insights / News
CMS-driven landing page with reusable article template.
Support future articles, company news, leadership perspectives, project updates, industry insights, and announcements.
Keep article presentation consistent and scalable.
Contact Us
Professional, concise, accessible, and reassuring.
Support General Contact and Project / Partnership Inquiry pathways.
Do not publish unapproved personal or temporary contact details.
Privacy Policy & Terms
Use clean legal-page typography, section anchors where useful, and generous readability.
Do not materially rewrite legal text for aesthetics or brevity.

15. RESPONSIVE DESIGN & LAYOUT
The website must function professionally across:
Desktop
Laptop
Tablet
Mobile
Mobile users should receive materially equivalent content and functionality.
Use responsive grids and fluid spacing rather than fixed desktop assumptions.
Adapt process graphics, cards, tables, menus, and CTAs for smaller screens.
Preserve content hierarchy and readability across breakpoints.
Avoid layouts that depend on hover alone for essential information.
16. TYPOGRAPHY & UI SYSTEM
Establish a consistent heading scale and body-text system.
Use readable line lengths and sufficient contrast.
Keep buttons, links, cards, form fields, icons, and section spacing consistent.
Use premium, restrained iconography rather than decorative clip-art or inconsistent icon families.
Maintain visual consistency across all nine page types and reusable CMS templates.
17. ANIMATION & INTERACTION
Animation should be subtle, smooth, purposeful, and lightweight.
Avoid:
Splash screens
Aggressive zoom effects
Excessive parallax
Constant motion
Flashing effects
Long intro animations
Respect reduced-motion preferences where applicable.
