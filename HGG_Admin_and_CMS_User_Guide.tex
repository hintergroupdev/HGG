\section{How to Generate Your Corporate Email App Password}\label{sec:app-password}

The Contact Us form is integrated with an automated SMTP mailer. Please follow the instructions below depending on your corporate email provider:

\subsection{For Google Workspace / Gmail (Recommended)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{Step \arabic*:}]
    \item Log into your corporate Google Account and navigate to \href{https://myaccount.google.com/security}{\textbf{Google Account Security Settings}} (\url{https://myaccount.google.com/security}).
    \item Ensure that \textbf{2-Step Verification} is turned \textbf{ON}. (If not, enable it using your phone).
    \item In the search bar at the top of your Google Account page, type: \texttt{App Passwords} and press Enter.
    \item In the \textbf{"App name"} field, enter: \texttt{HGG Website Contact Form} and click \textbf{Create}.
    \item Google will display a \textbf{16-character code} (e.g., \texttt{abcd efgh ijkl mnop}).
    \item \textbf{Copy this 16-character code} and send it to the developer along with your receiving email address (e.g., \texttt{info@hintergroupghana.com}).
\end{enumerate}

\begin{TipBox}{Security Guarantee}
App Passwords grant permission \emph{only} for outbound email dispatch through the Contact form. They do not give access to your Google Drive, calendar, or private account settings, and you can revoke them at any time with a single click.
\end{TipBox}

\subsection{For Microsoft 365 / Outlook Corporate Email}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{Step \arabic*:}]
    \item Log into your \href{https://mysignins.microsoft.com/security-info}{\textbf{Microsoft Security Info Page}} (\url{https://mysignins.microsoft.com/security-info}).
    \item Click \textbf{Add sign-in method} $\rightarrow$ Select \textbf{App password}.
    \item Enter the name: \texttt{HGG Corporate Web Form} and click \textbf{Next}.
    \item Copy the generated password and provide it to the developer.
\end{enumerate}
